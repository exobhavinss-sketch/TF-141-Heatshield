# Legacy chart validation against the master CSV

Reviewed the ten PNGs under `Datasets/charts/` without changing them. The review compares displayed metrics to `edadv/outputs/` summaries and the audited master CSV. A numerical match supports only the plotted synthetic records; it does not validate real conditions or encoded scores.

| File | Status | Validation and required action |
|---|---|---|
| `01_temperature_distribution.png` | Supported | Displayed pooled means (34.3 °C air and 35.9 °C LST) and synthetic disclosure agree with the audited summaries (34.28 and 35.89 °C). Retain as a descriptive distribution chart. |
| `02_monthly_seasonal_trends.png` | Needs correction | The displayed May LST peak (43.58 °C) matches the station-mean summary (43.581 °C). “Climatological,” named monsoon interpretation, and rain/cooling explanatory language are not supported by documented season definitions or real observations. Re-title using source month/season labels and remove mechanism claims. |
| `03_correlation_heatmap.png` | Needs correction | Displayed air–LST (~0.94), air–humidity (~−0.32), and LST–solar (~0.59) magnitudes are consistent with the Phase 2 correlation output at displayed precision. “Biophysical drivers” implies explanatory direction; describe these as synthetic-record associations and include paired n/dependence caveat. |
| `04_ndvi_vs_lst_scatter.png` | Needs correction | Displayed Pearson r≈−0.31 agrees with the Phase 2 value −0.307. “Demonstrating evaporative cooling benefits” is a causal/intervention claim that the observationally structured synthetic records cannot support. Retain the association only; independently reproduce the displayed regression slope before reusing it. |
| `05_builtup_intensity_vs_lst.png` | Needs correction | Both fields exist, but the displayed slope (+0.09 per index unit) is not listed in Phase 2 summaries and has not been independently reproduced in the validation outputs. “Anthropogenic thermal amplification” is an unsupported mechanism claim. Recompute with documented units/model or omit the slope and use associative language. |
| `06_heat_risk_distribution.png` | Further investigation | Category bar counts match the encoded-label totals: Low 21,737; Moderate 77,145; High 37,548; Very High 13,570. The seasonal composition of High/Very High is also reproducible from the CSV (26,768 Summer/52.4%; 13,874 Monsoon/27.1%; 8,943 Winter/17.5%; 1,533 Post-Monsoon/3.0%). However, risk thresholds and source-season definitions are unavailable; label the groups source-encoded, not validated heat-risk classes or validated climatological seasons. |
| `07_geographic_hotspot_map.png` | Needs correction | The master has 100 valid unique coordinates and the point mapping is spatially reproducible from station aggregates. Rename “hotspot” and “cooling priority” as simulated/encoded point summaries; these are not verified sensor sites, and a point map cannot establish unsampled-area hotspots. |
| `08_cooling_priority_ranking.png` | Supported | Top displayed mean scores (71.5 down through 64.8) match the descending station means in `station_aggregates.csv` at one-decimal precision. Describe them as source-encoded simulated scores; do not present them as validated municipal priorities. |
| `09_location_zone_comparison.png` | Supported | Zone-level LST ranking agrees with Phase 2 station means (Industrial 38.47 °C; Waterbody 29.48 °C). Shade/canopy fields exist and the grouped station aggregate values are reproducible. Interpret as descriptive comparisons of represented synthetic stations only. |
| `10_executive_summary_dashboard.png` | Further investigation | Audited values support the displayed 150,000 records, 100 stations, maximum LST 55.35 °C (shown 55.4), May mean LST 43.58 °C, mean encoded UHI 3.27 °C, 34.1% High/Very High encoded labels, and maximum encoded cooling potential near 4.05 °C. Intervention frequency is reproducible from categorical counts. Keep the simulation disclosure prominent and do not imply the cooling-potential field is measured/validated intervention impact. |

The chart review is a claim-level review, not a pixel-level reconstruction of every legacy plot. For unreported fitted slopes or visual encodings, see the individual row’s required follow-up. The final report uses the reproducible Phase 2 figures and outputs as its primary evidence.
